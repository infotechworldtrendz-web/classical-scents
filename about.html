Est. Karachi, 2019

Perfume made slowly, worn for a long time

Classical Scents began on a kitchen table in DHA with twelve glass vials and a conviction that Pakistan deserved a perfume house of its own.

The Beginning

From attar bazaar to atelier

Our founder grew up two streets from an attar shop in Saddar, where perfume was sold by the tola and dabbed from glass rods. The scents were extraordinary; the bottles were not. Classical Scents exists to put that depth of fragrance into something you would be proud to keep on your dresser.

Every formula is composed in Karachi, macerated for six weeks and bottled by hand in batches of two hundred.

What we stand for

Three promises on every bottle

Extrait concentration

Thirty percent fragrance oil in every spray, three times the strength of a typical eau de toilette. You use less and it stays longer.

Honest materials

Real Assam oud, Taif rose and Mysore sandalwood where we say so. No borrowed formulas, no diluted copies of other houses.

Made for this climate

Tested through Karachi summers and Lahore winters. If a scent cannot survive a June afternoon, it does not leave the atelier.

Process

How an extrait is born

  1. 01
    Brief

    A mood, a place, an occasion. Royal started as “the walk from the car to a valima in December”.

  2. 02
    Composition

    Forty to sixty trials over three months, worn on skin, not smelled on paper.

  3. 03
    Maceration

    Six weeks in the dark so the materials marry and the base notes learn to stay.

  4. 04
    Bottling

    Filtered, filled and sealed by hand in Karachi, then shipped within 24 hours of your order.
