Our Craft

The Art of Classical Perfumery

We compose slowly, in small batches, the way attar houses in the subcontinent have for two centuries. Every extrait rests for six weeks before it is bottled, so the top notes settle and the base notes learn to stay.

No borrowed formulas, no diluted sprays. Just concentrated perfume that lasts a Karachi day and still means something by night.
