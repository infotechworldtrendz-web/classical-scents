Help

Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take, and what does it cost?

Orders ship within 24 hours from Karachi. Karachi deliveries arrive in 1 to 2 working days; Lahore, Islamabad and other cities in 2 to 4. Delivery is Rs. 250, free on orders above Rs. 5,000.

How do I track my order?

You receive a courier tracking number on WhatsApp and email as soon as your parcel is booked. Reply to that message, or send your order number to +92 300 1234567, and we will check its status for you.

What is the exchange policy?

Unopened, sealed bottles can be exchanged within 15 days of delivery for another fragrance or size. Opened bottles cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, which is why we recommend the Discovery Set or a visit to the atelier if you are unsure.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery nationwide, plus bank transfer, JazzCash and Easypaisa. Advance payments get an extra 5% off; the discount is applied when we confirm your order on WhatsApp.

Are your perfumes original compositions?

Yes. Every fragrance is composed in-house in Karachi. We do not sell copies, impressions or decants of other brands.

How long will an extrait last on skin?

Between 8 and 14 hours depending on the fragrance, your skin and the weather. Attars last longer still, often into the next day. Each product page shows a longevity meter.
